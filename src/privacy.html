Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Who we are

This service ("SayTheWord") is an automation operated by the owner of the Instagram account you interacted with. It replies to comments and sends direct messages containing links when you comment a keyword (such as "link") on a reel.

What data we collect

  • Instagram user ID (IGSID) — a scoped identifier Instagram assigns to the interaction between you and this account
  • Your public username (when available) — used to greet you in messages and maintain your contact entry
  • Your comment text on the triggering post — used only to match keywords
  • Interaction events — whether we replied, whether you tapped a message button, whether you opened the link

We do not collect your email, phone number, followers list, or message content beyond the keyword-matching described above. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

How we use it

  • To reply publicly to your comment and send you the private message you requested by commenting
  • To remember that we already served you for a given post, so you are not messaged repeatedly
  • To measure basic funnel statistics (comments → messages → link clicks) for the account owner

Link tracking

Buttons in our messages route through our own server so the account owner can count clicks. This records only that your user ID opened the link — no IP addresses, device data, or browsing history are stored.

Data retention

Contact entries, interaction records and activity logs are kept until you ask us to delete them, or until the account owner removes them. Activity logs are automatically trimmed after 90 days.
