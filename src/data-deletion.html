Data Deletion

When you comment a keyword on our reels, we store your Instagram user ID, your public username, your comment text, and basic interaction records so we can send you the message you asked for. Here is how to have it deleted.

Fastest option: open your DM with the account and send delete my data — everything tied to your Instagram ID is erased immediately, confirmed by reply

Option 1 — Self-service in your DMs

1

Open your DM conversation with the account — find it in your Instagram inbox; it is the message you received after commenting

2

Send the message delete my data

3

You receive a confirmation reply, and everything tied to your Instagram ID is erased immediately

Option 2 — Contact the account owner

Send a direct message to the Instagram account you interacted with requesting deletion, or email the owner at the address they provide in their bio. We process manual requests within 30 days, usually much faster.

What gets deleted

  • Your contact entry (Instagram user ID and username)
  • All message-flow records tied to you
  • All activity-log entries tied to you

What stays untouched

Your Instagram account itself is unaffected — deletion here only removes data held by this service. Removing the app in your Instagram settings (Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites) also revokes our access, and you can request a copy or deletion of Meta-side data through Instagram's own "Manage your data" tools.
